01 / SKIN BEAUTY

Skin beauty, made simple.

A few thoughtful makeup techniques for a fresh, comfortable finish—adapt each one to your own routine.

  1. 01

    Prep, then pause

    Apply the moisturizer you enjoy and let it settle before makeup, so your base glides on smoothly.

  2. 02

    Start at the center

    Use a small amount of base and blend outward in light layers, adding only where you want coverage.

  3. 03

    Soften the edges

    Tap concealer only where you want it, then blend the edges with a clean finger or tool.
